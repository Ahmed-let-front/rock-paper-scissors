# Front-end Style Guide

## Layout

The designs were created to the following widths:

- Mobile: 320px - 375px+
- Desktop: 1366px

> 💡 Ensure content is fully responsive and meets WCAG requirements by testing across the complete range of screen sizes from 320px to large screens.

## Colors

### Primary

- Scissors (Gold): hsl(39 89% 49%) to hsl(40 84% 53%) (Edge: hsl(32 72% 42%))
- Paper (Blue): hsl(230 89% 62%) to hsl(230 89% 65%) (Edge: hsl(230 66% 46%))
- Rock (Red): hsl(349 71% 52%) to hsl(349 70% 56%) (Edge: hsl(349 70% 38%))

### Neutral & UI Tokens

- Dark Text: hsl(229 25% 31%)
- Score Text: hsl(229 64% 46%)
- Header Outline: hsl(217 16% 55%)
- Surface: hsl(0 0% 100%)

### Background

- Light Mode Canvas: hsl(214 45% 96%) to hsl(220 35% 84%)
- Dark Mode Canvas: hsl(214 47% 23%) to hsl(237 49% 15%)

## Fonts

- Family: [Barlow Semi Condensed](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Semi+Condensed)
- Weights: 600, 700
